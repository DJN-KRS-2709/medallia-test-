# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** RouteLogic Velocity (B2B)
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** If we do nothing for 12 months, RouteLogic keeps losing mid-market freight accounts to leaner dispatch tools at renewal. Renewals in the 50 to 200 truck segment already slipped from 91% to 84% this year, and every lost account gives sales the same reason: too many clicks to get a truck moving.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** The user is currently forced to keep a shared spreadsheet open next to RouteLogic, because rebooking one late delivery takes 14 screens. Priya, a dispatch coordinator at Harbor Line Freight, copies the order into the sheet, calls the driver, and keys everything back into RouteLogic at the end of her shift.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** We must stop losing mid-market renewals to leaner rivals by removing the side spreadsheet coordinators keep open just to rebook a late delivery.
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** For dispatch coordinators at mid-market freight accounts, we will let them rebook a late delivery from one screen in under a minute, because our largest segment renews in Q1 and the spreadsheet is the reason they give for leaving.

## Cold-read your own hook
- **Is the business risk high enough to justify a new initiative, a high-stakes threat, or a minor inconvenience?:** High-stakes. Renewals in our biggest segment dropped 7 points in a year, and the Q1 renewal wave decides whether that becomes a trend.
- **Is the moment of misery a systemic problem or just an edge case?:** Systemic, as far as I can tell. Every coordinator I'd expect to ask runs some version of the side spreadsheet. I only have Priya's story so far, so that's the claim to check first.
- **Does the value proposition actually remove the obstacle you identified, or is it a generic feature?:** Yes. One-screen rebooking removes the reason the spreadsheet exists. A faster search or a nicer dashboard wouldn't.
