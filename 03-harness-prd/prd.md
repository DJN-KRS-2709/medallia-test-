The gaps below come from the build notes above only. Only one of the six harness surfaces, 05 Permissions, has anything specified.

| # | Spec element | Gap | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Audit source (`02-prototype/prototype.md`) | The file and the prototype are missing, so nothing in these notes could be checked against the build. | **Prototype:** commit the prototype and `prototype.md` to `02-prototype/`, then rerun this audit against them. |
